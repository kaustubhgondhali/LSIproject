Income Replacement

1. Life Insurance & Term Protection

Protect your family's financial independence against life's unpredictable uncertainties.

The primary function of life insurance is not investment returns—it is pure financial risk replacement. If the primary earning member is absent, adequate term insurance ensures that liabilities (loans, mortgages) are cleared, and the family's living standard and children's education remain undisturbed.

  • Pure Term Plans: Maximum financial cover at cost-effective premiums.
  • Human Life Value (HLV): Calculating coverage based on 10x to 15x your annual income.
  • Debt & Mortgage Coverage: Ensuring family assets are never lost to lender claims.
Inquire on Term Insurance

The Term Insurance Rule of Thumb

Always keep insurance and investment separate. Pure term insurance provides substantial protection, allowing you to invest the difference into compounding mutual fund SIPs.

Coverage Formula Required Cover = (Annual Income × 15) + Outstanding Debts - Existing Assets

Healthcare Security

2. Health & Critical Illness Insurance

Shield your hard-earned wealth and investments from unexpected hospitalization bills.

Medical inflation in India currently exceeds 12-14% annually. A single major medical emergency can severely deplete years of family savings. Adequate individual and family floater health policies protect your balance sheet while giving your family access to quality medical care.

  • Family Floater Policies: Comprehensive coverage for self, spouse, and dependent children.
  • Super Top-Up Plans: High sum insured protection at minimal additional expense.
  • Pre & Post Hospitalization: Coverage for diagnostics, medication, and recovery expenses.
Evaluate Health Cover

Why Health Insurance Comes First

Defends Your Investment Portfolio
Prevents premature liquidation of long-term mutual fund SIPs and equity investments during illness.
Cashless Hospitalization Network
Direct settlement with network hospitals without out-of-pocket cash strain.
No Claim Bonus (NCB) Accumulation
Increases your total coverage every claim-free year at no extra cost.

Asset Protection

3. Motor & General Insurance

Protect vehicles, property, business establishments, and transit against accidents and damages.

Motor Insurance Solutions

Comprehensive and third-party protection for two-wheelers, private four-wheelers, and commercial fleet operations.

  • Third-Party Liability: Statutory compliance protecting against damages to third parties.
  • Comprehensive Own-Damage: Covers theft, fire, floods, and collision damages.
  • Add-on Covers: Zero Depreciation, Engine Protect, Roadside Assistance, and Consumables.

General & Property Insurance

Safeguard physical business establishments, shop inventories, residential homes, and commercial cargo from operational perils.

  • Shopkeeper & Business Insurance: Protects inventory, machinery, and structure against fire and theft.
  • Home Insurance: Covers residential building structure and valuable household contents.
  • Marine & Transit Cover: Protection for goods transported via road, rail, air, or sea.

Holistic Shield

5. The 3-Tier Family Protection Architecture

Wealth creation cannot survive without a solid protective foundation.

Before aggressive market investing, every household must build a three-layer defensive fortress. If a crisis occurs, the fortress absorbs the impact so your long-term wealth compounding remains untouched.

Tier 1: Emergency Fund Shield
6 months of essential household expenses stored in liquid funds or bank deposits.
Tier 2: Comprehensive Health Floater
Adequate hospital coverage for all family members to prevent medical debt.
Tier 3: Pure Term Life Insurance
Adequate HLV replacement ensuring financial freedom for your dependents.

1. LEARN

3

Disciplined Wealth Creation Roadmap

CASE STUDY: CHILD EDUCATION FUND
Building a Higher Education Corpus through a ₹5,000 SIP

A parent from Uran initiated a monthly SIP of ₹5,000 when their child was 5 years old. Over a disciplined 12-year horizon, rupee cost averaging helped build a resilient fund that funded professional college education comfortably.

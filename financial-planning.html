Diagnostic Phase

1. Complete Financial Assessment

Understand your true financial standing before deploying capital into the market.

A disciplined financial plan starts with complete transparency on your cash inflows, mandatory expenses, debt commitments, and existing asset valuations. We help you create a unified family balance sheet that identifies leaks and unlocks surplus capital for systematic wealth creation.

  • Cash Flow Analysis: Tracking surplus savings rate as a percentage of monthly income.
  • Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio: Ensuring loan obligations remain within sustainable limits.
  • Net Worth Tracking: Evaluating liquid versus illiquid assets for true solvency.

Psychology & Capacity

2. Scientific Risk Profiling

Aligning your investment portfolio with both your financial capacity and psychological temperament.

Risk tolerance is not just about wanting high returns—it is about your ability to endure temporary market drawdowns without panicking. We categorize learners across three primary risk archetypes to ensure portfolio choices never cause emotional distress.

  • Conservative: Focus on capital protection, debt funds, and hybrid allocations.
  • Moderate / Balanced: Balanced growth with equity and debt hybrid stability.
  • Aggressive / Growth: Long-term equity bias (5-20 yrs) targeting wealth compounding.

Tax Efficiency

3. Tax-Saving & Efficiency Strategies

Structure your investments legitimately under prevailing tax provisions to maximize post-tax compounding.

ELSS Tax-Saving Funds

Equity Linked Savings Schemes offer tax deductions under applicable sections with the shortest lock-in period (3 years) among 80C options.

Health Insurance Deductions

Medical insurance premiums for self, spouse, children, and senior citizen parents provide dual benefits of protection and statutory deductions.

Long-Term Capital Gains Planning

Holding equity investments beyond 12 months enables favorable capital gains tax treatment compared to short-term speculative trading.

4. Retirement Planning

Plan ahead for inflation-protected, financially stress-free golden years. Calculate your post-retirement monthly expenses and build an adequate capital corpus.

  • Corpus Estimation: Factoring in 6-7% annual living inflation over 25+ retirement years.
  • SWP Cash Flow: Transitioning from active salary to systematic monthly distributions.
  • Healthcare Buffer: Dedicated medical emergency funds separate from monthly pensions.

5. Investment Planning

Tie every rupee invested to a tangible life milestone instead of random speculation. Ensure the right holding period for every asset class.

Portfolio Discipline

6. Asset Allocation & Rebalancing

"Asset allocation accounts for over 90% of long-term portfolio return variability."

Rather than putting all capital into a single asset class, a balanced portfolio spreads risk across Equity (growth), Debt (stability), Gold (inflation hedge), and Liquid Cash (emergencies). Periodic annual rebalancing locks in gains from outperforming assets and buys undervalued assets automatically.

Equity (50% - 70%)
Beats long-term inflation and compounds wealth.
Debt (20% - 35%)
Cushions market volatility and provides liquidity.
Gold (5% - 10%)
Macro hedge during geopolitical uncertainties.
Liquid Cash (6 Mos)
Immediate crisis shield without portfolio liquidation.

Course Overview

What is Share Market Education & Training?

Share Market Education & Training is the complete, single flagship program offered by Lord Sai Investment & Share Market Academy in Uran. It takes you from foundational capital market mechanics all the way to advanced chart analysis, risk control mathematics, and live market execution.

Unlike fragmented workshops or isolated video tutorials, this integrated course brings every essential dimension of trading and wealth compounding together under one unified, sequential learning path.

Zero-Fluff Foundations
Understand exchanges, order types, demat operations, and market participants.
Price Action Mastery
Read candlesticks, trends, and support-resistance without relying on tips.
Multi-Style Strategies
Master intraday momentum, swing trading, options hedging, and long-term SIP compounding.
Risk Mathematics

Unified Learning Philosophy

Why One Complete Course Beats Random Tips

True confidence in financial markets is not built by chasing random stock tips or watching disconnected YouTube videos. It comes from having a complete, structured framework that connects:

  • Market Mechanics — Knowing how orders execute, who moves prices, and how liquidity works.
  • Chart Reading — Identifying high-probability setups across multiple timeframes.
  • Position Sizing Mathematics — Calculating exact share quantities before entering any position.
  • Emotional Discipline — Sticking to a written trading plan and maintaining a verified trade journal.

Complete Curriculum

What You Will Learn in This Course

The 9 core learning modules integrated into the Share Market Education & Training program.

Stage 1: Foundation Stage 2: Analysis Stage 3: Strategy Stage 4: Wealth Stage 5: Discipline Stage 6: Practical
Module 01 Stage: Foundation
Stock Market Basics

Master capital market operations, exchange mechanics, and account navigation.

  • NSE & BSE Exchange Structure
  • Shares, Equity & IPO Fundamentals
  • Demat & Trading Account Operations
  • Market Participants (FII, DII, Retail)
  • Order Types (Market, Limit, Stop-Loss)
  • Delivery vs Intraday Distinctions
Learning Outcome: Operate demat accounts independently without confusion.
Module 02 Stage: Analysis
Technical Analysis & Price Action

Learn the universal visual language of candlestick charts and market trend structures.

  • Candlestick Anatomy & Buyer-Seller Psychology
  • Single & Multi-bar Candlestick Patterns
  • Dynamic Support & Resistance Zones
  • Trendlines, Channels & Chart Patterns
  • Volume Spread Confirmation Analysis
  • Multi-Timeframe Top-Down Confirmation
Learning Outcome: Read charts and spot high-probability entries independently.
Module 03 Stage: Strategy
Intraday Trading Strategies

Develop structured, same-day trade execution skills with strict loss limits.

  • Opening Range & Market Structure Breakouts
  • High-Probability Same-Day Trade Setups
  • Precision Entry & Systematic Exit Rules
  • Minimum 1:2 Risk-Reward Ratio Framework
  • Intraday Position Sizing Mathematics
  • Live Trade Management & Trailing Stop Loss
Learning Outcome: Execute intraday setups with disciplined risk containment.
Module 04 Stage: Strategy
Swing Trading (Working Professionals)

Capture multi-day and multi-week trends with post-market evening analysis.

  • Daily & Weekly Chart Trend Mapping
  • Breakout & Retest Entry Confirmations
  • High-Probability Swing Pattern Formations
  • End-of-Day Order Placement Protocols
  • Swing Capital Allocation & Position Sizing
  • Managing Positions with Minimal Screen Time
Learning Outcome: Trade larger positional moves part-time without watching live ticks.
Module 05 Stage: Advanced
Options Trading & Derivatives

Demystify Call/Put options, Greeks, option chain analysis, and hedging concepts.

  • Call & Put Options Fundamentals
  • Strike Price Selection (ITM, ATM, OTM)
  • Option Greeks (Delta, Theta, Gamma, Vega)
  • Option Chain & Open Interest (OI) Decoding
  • Directional & Defined-Risk Hedging Concepts
  • Avoiding Common Out-of-the-Money Traps
Learning Outcome: Master derivatives math and risk-defined option setups.
Module 06 Stage: Wealth
Investment & Wealth Compounding

Learn long-term equity investing, mutual funds, SIPs, and retirement blueprints.

  • Trading vs Investing Wealth Mindset
  • The Mathematics of Long-Term Compounding
  • Portfolio Diversification & Asset Allocation
  • Mutual Fund Categories & Selection Criteria
  • SIP & Index ETF Compounding Frameworks
  • Goal-Based Investing & Retirement Corpus Sizing
Learning Outcome: Design multi-year compounding portfolios for life goals.
Module 07 Stage: Discipline
Capital Protection & Risk Rules

The core survival framework for capital preservation and trading consistency.

  • Strict 1% - 2% Risk-Per-Trade Formula
  • Mathematical Position Sizing Calculator
  • Hard & Trailing Stop Loss Protocols
  • Maximum Daily Loss & Drawdown Limits
  • Trade Risk vs Total Portfolio Risk
  • Drawdown Recovery & Capital Preservation
Learning Outcome: Protect trading capital from blowups with mathematical discipline.
Module 08 Stage: Discipline
Trading Psychology & Mindset

Conquer fear, greed, FOMO, and emotional impulses to trade with calm consistency.

  • Overcoming Fear & Greed Emotional Cycles
  • Eliminating FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)
  • Preventing Overtrading & Revenge Traps
  • Writing & Executing a Clear Trading Plan
  • Handling Losing Streaks Professionally
  • Maintaining a Verifiable Digital Trade Journal
Learning Outcome: Transform from an emotional decision-maker into a calm, rule-based trader.
Module 09 Stage: Practical
Live Market Observation

Experience live chart dynamics, market structure recognition, and debriefs in real time.

  • Real-Time Candlestick & Volume Dynamics
  • Plotting Live Support & Resistance Zones
  • Pre-Market Routine & Scenario Planning
  • Live Risk Management Application
  • Post-Market Analysis & Session Debrief
  • Mistake Review & Practical Learning Log
Learning Outcome: Bridge theory and real-time market execution without fear.
